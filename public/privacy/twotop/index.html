Privacy Policy for Two-Top

Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Two-Top: Games for Two handles information when you play.

Overview

Two-Top is a collection of games for two people on one iPhone or iPad. It plays fully offline, needs no account, and collects no data. It has no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking across apps or websites.

Your Game Data

Your game settings (categories, match length, timer, difficulty, seating and any player names you type), your recent play history (so matches don't repeat questions) and any questions you flag are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. Apple may include this data in device backups, depending on your backup settings; Apple handles backup data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Questions Written on Your Device

On iPhone and iPad models with Apple Intelligence, Two-Top can use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework to write a few extra trivia questions. This happens entirely on your device, and nothing is sent to a server to make them. You can turn Fresh questions off in the game setup screen.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information through Two-Top. The app does not include:

  • Advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Account registration or sign-in
  • Network access of any kind
  • Cross-app tracking

Data Retention

Your game data stays on your device until you delete the app or erase the device.

Children's Privacy

Two-Top does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.
