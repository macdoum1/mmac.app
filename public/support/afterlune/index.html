Afterlune is an offline anime adventure for iPhone. You buy it once and it's all yours: there are no in-app purchases, no ads and no account.

Getting Started

Tap the title screen to start in the glade. Use the stick to move, and the jump button to jump. In the air, tap jump again (it turns into a crescent) to open the moon veil and glide. Step through the moon gate to visit the Driftisles and gather Moonglass before moonset.

Summons

Summons use only Moonglass you earn by playing. Nothing can be bought, and the odds are always shown on the summon screen.

Saving

The game saves on its own, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, so keep the app installed (or keep a device backup) if you want to keep your heroes.

Comfort and Accessibility

Settings (the gear) has text size, reduce motion, haptics, sound volumes, a frame rate choice of 30, 60 or 120 fps (30 saves battery) and a left-handed layout. The game plays in portrait or landscape.

Internet and Privacy

Afterlune works without an internet connection and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
