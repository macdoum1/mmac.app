Privacy Policy for Afterlune

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Afterlune handles information when you play.

Overview

Afterlune is a single-player game that plays fully offline. It makes no network requests, needs no account, and collects no data. It has no advertising, no analytics, no in-app purchases and no tracking across apps or websites.

Your Game Data

Your progress (heroes, Moonglass, summons and daily rewards) and your settings (sound, text size, haptics, frame rate and similar) are saved in the app's storage on your device. They are never sent to me or to anyone else. Apple may include this data in device backups, depending on your backup settings; Apple handles backup data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Photos

Photo mode only hides the game's buttons so you can take a screenshot with your phone. The game does not access your photo library.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any information through Afterlune. The app does not include:

  • Advertising or analytics SDKs
  • Account registration or sign-in
  • Network access of any kind
  • Cross-app tracking

Data Retention

Your game data stays on your device until you delete the app or erase the device.

Children's Privacy

Afterlune does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact
