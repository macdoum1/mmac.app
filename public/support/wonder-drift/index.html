Wonder Drift Support

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Wonder Drift is a calm iPhone app of painted, gently animated places. Swipe up to drift to another place, tap the water for a ripple, and use the bookmark to keep a postcard you'd like to revisit.

Sound

The speaker button turns scene ambience on and off, and the music note turns generative music on and off. Both start off, play alongside audio from other apps, and keep playing with the screen locked. While sound is on, the moon button sets a sleep timer of 15, 30 or 60 minutes; the sound fades out over the last minute.

Saved Postcards

Tap the stack icon at the top right to see your saved postcards, and tap one to return to it in the same light and weather. Swipe left on a postcard, or tap Edit, to remove it.

Sharing

Tap the share button to send a still image of the current place, with its name.

Motion and Accessibility

If the motion is too much, turn on Reduce Motion in Settings > Accessibility > Motion; Wonder Drift pauses its scene animation while it is on. VoiceOver describes each place, and the text follows your preferred text size.

Internet and Privacy

Wonder Drift works without an internet connection and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
