Two-Top Support

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Two-Top is a collection of games for two people and one iPhone or iPad: trivia, word races, reflex duels and board games. Each player gets their own half of the screen, turned to face them. Every game also has a solo mode. It works offline, with no ads, no account and no subscriptions.

Getting Started

Tap a game on the home screen, choose two players or solo, and tap Play. Set the phone between you. Tap the pause button in the middle of the screen to pause, switch seating or end the match. After a match you can rematch in one tap, keep a running series and swap seats.

How to Play

Each game's setup screen explains its rules, and new games arrive in app updates. In board games you can play a friend, or play solo against a bot at the difficulty you choose.

Seating

Pick Face to face when the phone lies flat between you across a table, and Side by side when you're next to each other, like on a bench or in airplane seats. You can switch mid-match from the pause menu.

AI Questions

On iPhone and iPad models with Apple Intelligence, the trivia game can write a few extra questions on your device. They're checked before you see them, labeled "Fresh" and limited to a quarter of a match. If one looks wrong, tap Report and it won't come back. You can turn AI questions off in the trivia setup.

Found a Mistake?

Sorry about that. Email the game, what happened and what you expected, such as a trivia answer or a word that wasn't accepted, and it'll be fixed in an update.

Internet and Privacy

Two-Top works without an internet connection and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
