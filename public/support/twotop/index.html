Two-Top Support

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Two-Top is three games for two people and one iPhone or iPad: Trivia Face-Off, Alphabet Soup and Four Up. Each player gets their own half of the screen, turned to face them. It works offline, with no ads, no account and no subscriptions.

Getting Started

Tap a game on the home screen, choose Two players or Solo, and tap Play. Set the phone between you. Tap the pause button in the middle of the screen to pause, switch seating or end the match. After a match you can rematch in one tap, keep a running series and swap seats.

Trivia Face-Off

Both players see the same question. The first correct tap scores a point. A wrong tap locks you out of that question, and your opponent gets a free shot. Ties go to sudden death. In Solo, the questions you miss are saved so you can retry them.

Alphabet Soup

A crossword race. You both get the same letters. Drag them onto your board so every run of letters across and down is a real word of three or more letters, all joined in one grid. The first player to use every letter wins. If the clock runs out first, the player with more letters in finished words wins. Tap a placed tile to send it back, or use Shuffle and Clear on the rack. Any valid grid counts, and the results show one answer.

Four Up

Take turns dropping discs. The first to line up four across, up or diagonally wins, and a full board is a draw. Play a friend, or play Solo against an Easy, Sharp or Shark bot. With the optional shot clock, a disc drops in a random column when time runs out.

Seating

Pick Face to face when the phone lies flat between you across a table, and Side by side when you're next to each other, like on a bench or in airplane seats. You can switch mid-match from the pause menu.

AI Questions

On iPhone and iPad models with Apple Intelligence, Trivia Face-Off can write a few extra questions on your device. They're checked before you see them, labeled "Fresh" and limited to a quarter of a match. If one looks wrong, tap Report and it won't come back. You can turn AI questions off in Trivia setup.

Found a Mistake?

Sorry about that. Email the trivia question and the answer you expected, or the word Alphabet Soup didn't accept, and it'll be fixed in an update.

Internet and Privacy

Two-Top works without an internet connection and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
