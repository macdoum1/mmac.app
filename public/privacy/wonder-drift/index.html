Privacy Policy for Wonder Drift

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Wonder Drift handles information when you use the app.

Overview

Wonder Drift does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. It has no account, no advertising, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not contact a server or track you across apps or websites.

Saved Postcards

When you save a postcard, Wonder Drift stores a small description of that scene (which place, its layout, and its light and weather) in the app's storage on your device so it can show it again. It is not sent anywhere. Apple may include it in device backups, depending on your backup settings; Apple handles backup data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Sound

Scene ambience and music are synthesized live on your device. Wonder Drift does not use the microphone. Sound can keep playing while your screen is locked, until you turn it off or an optional sleep timer ends; this happens entirely on your device.

Sharing

If you share a postcard, Wonder Drift creates an image on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is up to you and the app you choose; Wonder Drift does not receive a copy or know who it was sent to.

On-Device Intelligence

On supported devices, Wonder Drift may use Apple's on-device language model to choose between prepared scene layouts. This runs entirely on your iPhone, and no text or scene data leaves it.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information through Wonder Drift.

The app does not include:

  • Third-party advertising SDKs
  • Third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • In-app account registration
  • Cross-app tracking

Data Retention

Saved postcards remain on your device until you remove them in the app, delete the app, or erase the device.

Children's Privacy

Wonder Drift does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when the latest changes were made.
