Last updated: November 15, 2026

Two-Top is a collection of games for two people and one iPhone or iPad, starting with Trivia Face-Off. It works offline, with no ads, no account and no subscriptions.

How to Play

Open Trivia Face-Off, choose Two players, and set the phone between you. Each player answers on their own half of the screen. The first correct tap scores a point. A wrong tap locks you out of that question, and your opponent gets a free shot. If the match ends in a tie, sudden death decides it.

Solo Practice

Choose Solo practice in setup to play on your own. At the end you'll see every question you missed, with a short fact about the answer.

Seating

Pick Face to face when the phone lies flat between you across a table, and Side by side when you're next to each other, like on a bench or in airplane seats. You can switch mid-match from the pause menu. On iPhone Duo, each player gets their own panel.

Fresh Questions

On iPhone and iPad models with Apple Intelligence, Two-Top can write a few extra questions on your device. They're checked before you see them, labeled "Fresh" and limited to a quarter of a match. If one looks wrong, tap "Bad question" and it won't come back. You can turn Fresh questions off in setup.

Found a Wrong Answer?

Sorry about that. Email the question and the answer you expected, and it'll be fixed in an update.

Internet and Privacy

Two-Top works without an internet connection and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
